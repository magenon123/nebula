5 reels x 5 rows. There are no paylines: make 3, 4 or 5 of the same symbol in a straight line (across, down or diagonal) and it pays. Every tile that wins is SEALED with gold and stays, all other tiles spin again. Each new link in the chain raises the multiplier. Max win [[maxWin]]x your bet.

SEALED CHAINAfter the first spin every winning tile gets a gold seal and keeps its symbol. The free tiles respin. A new stage pays only lines that contain at least one NEW tile. The chain ends when a stage brings no new win or the board is full.
CHAIN MULTIPLIERStage 1 pays x1, stage 2 pays x2, then x3, x4 and x5 for every later stage. Watch the CHAIN tag on the left.
DJINN SEAL (WILD)The blue seal with the crescent and star replaces every pay symbol. It lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 only and is sealed like any winning tile.
WISH GEMSGems with a number (x2 to x25) lock into place the moment they land and pay nothing by themselves. When the chain ends with a win, all gems on the board are added together and multiply the whole chain win: x2 + x5 = x7. No win, no gems.
FS (SCATTER)The golden sun with the letters FS pays nothing by itself. 3 FS on the first stage start FREE WISHES (10 spins), 4 FS start the SUPER round THREE WISHES (12 spins), 5 FS give 15 spins. 3 or more FS in the bonus add spins.
FREE WISHESThe chain multiplier never resets. It climbs by one with every winning stage across the whole bonus, up to x12 (x15 in the SUPER round, which starts at x3). Spins without a win do not touch it. Gems still multiply the end of each chain.
ASTROLABE OF WISHESThe brass instrument pays nothing on the board. 3, 4 or 5 Astrolabes on the first stage start the bonus with 4, 6 or 8 spins. Every spin the three rings stop one after another: the outer ring gives CASH, the middle ring a MULTIPLIER, and the core may light a WISH: MINI, MINOR, MAJOR or GRAND, paid on top. Spin prize = cash x multiplier (+ wish).
DJINN'S FAVOUR (BET-UP)Doubles the cost of a spin. FS and Astrolabes land about three times as often and Wish Gems sometimes arrive for free.
BONUS BUYFREE WISHES for [[buy:fs]]x your bet, ASTROLABE OF WISHES for [[buy:astro]]x, SUPER THREE WISHES for [[buy:super]]x. The reels drop the matching symbols and the bonus starts like a natural trigger. The round never pays more than [[maxWin]]x.

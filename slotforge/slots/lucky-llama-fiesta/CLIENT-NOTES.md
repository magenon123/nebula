# Lucky Llama Fiesta client notes (kai)
Files: slot.json (bets/maxWin/anteCost/buys equal the engine CFG; text, loading, fever "Fiesta Luck", buys, intro/outro), slot.js (hooks), kai.css (client css; `python3 embed-kai.py` splices it after the `/* KAI */` marker of slot.css, art builds keep it). Smoke: `node slotforge/shell/smoke-lucky-llama.cjs`. Build: `python3 build-standalone.py lucky-llama-fiesta --server`. Test handle: `window.__ll`.
Design: 15 real `.cell`s in #grid (idx = reel*3+row, grid placement kept in inline style) + `#reels` (5 clipped strips of 15 cells: pad, 3 final, 8 spin-blur (`sb*`), 3 old). The strip scrolls with translate3d only; a simulated clock (`simT += dt / T()`) drives everything so turbo and the tap speed-up also shorten a spin that is already running. Stop times are solved (planNormal) so the 430 ms quadratic landing ends at the planned instant (900 ms + 220 ms per reel); tease reels (planTease) ramp down to a slow speed with a heartbeat + glow. At touch the real cells (same symbols) replace the strip and bounce 11 px.
Rounds: `baseSpin(R)` stores R; base/trigger spin -> lines (callout plate #coLine "LINE 7 - 3x TACO - $2.50", thick colored path layer, `.cell.hit` via FX.act, others dim `#grid.focus`, wild cells ring `.wl`); more than 3 winning lines: top 2 singly, the rest grouped. Link = shell bonus loop over `bonus.spins` (respins), cracks inside the LAST respin (pays the total through ctx.onWin). Parade = free spins; sticky wilds stay as real cells (class `sticky top`) above the strips; ladder `#ladder.m<step>`; Collector grabs are NOT multiplied (callout says so). Class `pop` is the shell's floating-pill class: never put it on art plates, kai.css uses `bump`.
Pace (default speed): spin 0.9 s + 0.22 s/reel, line 0.9 s each, group 1.25 s, respin ~1.8 s, crack 0.36 s (0.26 s when > 9), ladder callout 1.5 s.

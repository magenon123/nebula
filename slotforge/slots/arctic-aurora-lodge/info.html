6 reels x 5 rows. There are no lines: collect 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the board and it pays by the total AREA you hold. Symbols land as single tiles or as giant ice blocks, a 2x2 block counts as 4 and a 3x3 block as 9. Blocks are one piece of ice, never cut off at the edge. Max win [[maxWin]]x your bet.

COLOSSAL ICE BLOCKSA 2x2 block of one symbol is worth 4 tiles, a 3x3 block is worth 9. A single 3x3 block already makes 9: an instant win. Nothing is removed and nothing falls, each spin is judged once.
AURORA SPIRIT (WILD)The glowing orb stands in for every pay symbol and counts as one tile. It appears as a single tile only, never inside a block.
FS (SCATTER)The ember with the letters FS pays nothing by itself. 3 FS anywhere start FREE SPINS (10 spins), 4 FS start 12. FS during the bonus add 5 spins.
FREE SPINS: AURORA MUSEBefore every free spin the aurora lights ONE symbol (shown on the meter at the left). For that symbol only 5 tiles are enough to win, and its giant blocks count double: a lit 3x3 block is worth 18. Nothing carries over to the next spin.
AURORA GEMThe cut green-violet gem pays nothing on the board. 4 or more Aurora Gems start the AURORA SWEEP. Watch the gem sockets on the left meter fill up.
AURORA SWEEPThe screen freezes into a 5x5 sheet of ice that hides prizes. The aurora sweeps across it, one glowing band per gem (4 to 8 sweeps): a band melts a whole row, column or diagonal. A cell melted by several bands pays its prize multiplied by the number of bands that crossed it, and a prism adds one more crossing. Total = the sum of all melted prizes.
NORTHERN LIGHTS (BET-UP)Doubles the cost of a spin. Giant blocks become much more common and the bonus symbols land about three times as often.
BONUS BUYFREE SPINS for [[buy:fs]]x your bet, AURORA SWEEP for [[buy:sweep]]x. The reels drop the matching symbols and the bonus starts like a natural trigger. The round never pays more than [[maxWin]]x.

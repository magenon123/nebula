CLOUDTOP TEA HOUSE · TIN RUSH

5 reels x 4 rows, 30 FIXED LINES (all always on). Three or more matching symbols in a row from the left on a line pay the line bet. The best win per line counts and all lines add up. Dango, Onigiri and Paper Fan need 3 or more as well, they just pay less. Max win 5,000x your bet.

SMILING KITE (WILD)Lands on reels 2, 3 and 4. It stands in for every pay symbol. It does not stand in for the Furoshiki bundle or the Tea Tin.
FUROSHIKI BUNDLE (MYSTERY)After the reels stop, every bundle on the board unties at the same moment and turns into the SAME symbol. One bundle can lift a line. Three or more make a big spin.
TEA TIN (BONUS)Tins pay nothing on the lines and never block a line. 6 or more Tins anywhere on the board open TIN RUSH. If 2 Tins have landed and the next reel is still falling, hold on to your cup.
TIN RUSHYour Tins stay in their drawers, each one carries a prize in multiples of your bet. You get 3 pours. Every pour that fills a new drawer resets the count to 3, a pour that fills nothing uses one up. The round ends when the pours run out or all 20 drawers are full.
KITE LAUNCHThe moment a row of 5 drawers is full, its gate lights and a kite takes off: every Tin in that row is doubled once, jackpot tins included.
SPECIAL TINSKoji's brass kettle (COLLECT) adds up all the Tins on the board when it lands. The bronze MINI, silver MINOR and gold MAJOR Tins pay 10x, 25x and 250x your bet.
GRAND DRAGON KITEFill all 20 drawers and the Dragon Kite flies: all Tins plus a 500x bonus. The round never pays more than 5,000x.
BONUS BUYBuy TIN RUSH for 60x your bet. The reels drop the Tins, then the round plays like a natural start. This is the only extra: there is no bet-up mode.

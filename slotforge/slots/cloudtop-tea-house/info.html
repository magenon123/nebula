5 reels x 4 rows, 30 FIXED LINES (all always on). Three or more matching symbols in a row from the left on a line pay the line bet. The best win per line counts and all lines add up. Dango, Onigiri and Paper Fan need 3 or more as well, they just pay less. Max win [[maxWin]]x your bet.

SMILING KITE (WILD)Lands on reels 2, 3 and 4. It stands in for every pay symbol. It does not stand in for the Furoshiki bundle or the Tea Tin.
FUROSHIKI BUNDLE (MYSTERY)After the reels stop, every bundle on the board unties at the same moment and turns into the SAME symbol. One bundle can lift a line. Three or more make a big spin.
TEA TIN (BONUS)Tins pay nothing on the lines and never block a line. 6 or more Tins anywhere on the board open TIN RUSH. If 2 Tins have landed and the next reel is still falling, hold on to your cup.
TIN RUSHYour Tins stay in their drawers, each one carries a prize in multiples of your bet. You get 3 pours. Every pour that fills a new drawer resets the count to 3, a pour that fills nothing uses one up. The round ends when the pours run out or all 20 drawers are full.
KITE LAUNCHThe moment a row of 5 drawers is full, its gate lights and a kite takes off: every Tin in that row is doubled once, jackpot tins included.
SPECIAL TINSKoji's brass kettle (COLLECT) adds up all the Tins on the board when it lands. The bronze MINI, silver MINOR and gold MAJOR Tins pay 10x, 25x and 250x your bet.
GRAND DRAGON KITEFill all 20 drawers and the Dragon Kite flies: all Tins plus a 500x bonus. The round never pays more than [[maxWin]]x.
FS DRUM (SCATTER)The taiko drum with the letters FS pays nothing on the lines and never blocks one. It can land on every reel. 3 FS anywhere start FREE SPINS, 4 FS start SUPER FREE SPINS, 5 FS start Super with extra spins. If 2 drums have landed and the next reel is still falling, hold on to your cup. If 6 Tea Tins and 3 FS land together, Tin Rush wins.
FREE SPINS spins, no extra cost. Tea Tins do not appear, the Smiling Kite shows up more often. Each of the 20 drawers remembers how strongly it has been steeped (see the table below).
STEEPING DRAWERSA drawer that takes part in a winning line gets steeped one level darker after the spin (once per spin, even on several lines). A later win through steeped drawers pays more: the line pays x(1 + the boosts of all its drawers). Levels stay until the bonus ends. The topmost level sends a paper kite into the sky.
SUPER FREE SPINS spins ( with 5 FS). Koji pre-steeps random drawers to level before the first spin. Five levels, gold drawers at the top, bigger boosts and more Smiling Kites. A very volatile bonus.
MORE SPINSFS drums inside a bonus add spins: in FREE SPINS, in SUPER. Drawer levels stay. A retrigger never turns FREE SPINS into SUPER. The bonus never lasts more than / spins.
BONUS BUYThree bonuses can be bought: TIN RUSH for [[buy:tin]]x your bet, FREE SPINS for [[buy:fs]]x and SUPER FREE SPINS for [[buy:super]]x. The reels drop the matching scatters, then the bonus plays like a natural start. There is no bet-up mode. All bonuses are capped by the [[maxWin]]x maximum win.

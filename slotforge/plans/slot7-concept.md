# Slot #7: LUCKY LLAMA FIESTA — concept (normal reel slot)
Slug: `lucky-llama-fiesta`. Status: CONCEPT, approved by the lead on the owner's behalf ("make a new normal one") under OWNER-PREFS rules. Next: ONE look-test picture, then engine / art / client.

**Hook:** Spin a Mexican-Andean street fiesta with Don Lucho the llama, collect money piñatas into a Hold & Win with four jackpots, and trigger Poncho Parade free spins where his wild ponchos stick and the multiplier climbs.
**Theme:** a sunny village fiesta (papel-picado banners, adobe walls, marigolds, lanterns, mariachi stage at dusk). No forge, undersea, tea, Nordic, Arabian or mine. Hero = Don Lucho, a proud llama in a embroidered poncho and sombrero, who lives ON the reels (high symbol, wild face, bonus star).

## Layout and win rule
- 5 reels x 3 rows, 20 fixed paylines, left to right, 3+ of a kind on adjacent reels; highest win per line, line wins add. Wilds substitute for everything except scatter and money.
- Spinning reels, stop left to right; 2 scatters on reels 1-3 slow-stop the remaining reels (tease).
- Volatility high. Hit frequency target ~28% (any win), ~1 in 130 spins for a bonus. Max win 7,500x. RTP 96.0-96.5% in every mode (engine person tunes by seeded sim).

## Symbols (15)
Low (5, painted fiesta objects on a tile): 1 **Marigold** (orange flower), 2 **Maracas** (red/green), 3 **Chili** (red pepper, curled), 4 **Guitar** (small guitarrón, wood), 5 **Taco** (stuffed, steaming).
Mid/high (4): 6 **Sugar Skull** (candy calavera, teal/pink), 7 **Sombrero** (gold-trim), 8 **Luchador Mask** (red/silver), 9 **Trumpet** (brass, mariachi).
10 **Don Lucho** (HERO, top pay: llama bust, cream fur, striped poncho, flower crown, gold tooth smile).
11 **WILD "Poncho"** (Lucho's striped poncho with WILD ribbon; substitutes; in free spins it sticks).
12 **SCATTER "Fiesta Drum"** (painted drum with FS plaque; reels 1-5).
13 **MONEY PIÑATA** (star piñata, shows a cash value x1-x50 bet on its belly; jackpot piñatas show MINI / MINOR / MAJOR / GRAND).
14 **Collector Piñata** (golden, bonus only, see free spins) and 15 **Lucky Coin Burst** (spark-fx on the money value only, not a pay symbol). (Count of paying/regular symbols: 13 distinct on reels.)

## Money feature: PIÑATA LINK (Hold & Win)
1. Money piñatas can land on any reel in the base game and free spins (value ranges x1-x25 bet; jackpots rare).
2. 6+ piñatas anywhere trigger LINK. They freeze in place, all others spin blank.
3. Player gets 3 respins. Every new piñata that lands freezes and resets respins to 3. A counter ("RESPINS 3") sits above the board.
4. Piñatas that land show their value popping up in the cell; at the end each piñata is "cracked" left to right, adding its value to a running total banner.
5. Jackpots by piñata: MINI 20x, MINOR 50x, MAJOR 250x, GRAND 2,000x (all x bet). Fill all 15 cells = GRAND awarded plus all values.
6. Link ends when respins reach 0; the total is paid.

## Free spins: PONCHO PARADE (3+ drums; 3 = 8 spins, 4 = 12, 5 = 20)
**Twist: sticky ponchos with a climbing ladder.** Every WILD that lands sticks for the rest of the bonus and Don Lucho adds a stripe to the win multiplier ladder: 1 wilds = x1, 2 = x2, 3 = x3, 5 = x5, 7 = x8, 10+ = x10, applied to ALL line wins every spin. 3 drums in the bonus add +3 spins. Piñatas in the bonus do NOT start Link, they give a mini cash-grab: Don Lucho swings and collects any value on screen (instant, shown as flying coins). Max free spins bonus can reach thousands x via the ladder.

## Extras
- **FIESTA LUCK** (bet-up, 2x bet): drums and piñatas land about 5-6x more often, the board shows a lit marquee, still ~96%. Always available toggle.
- **Bonus buys** (hanging sign on the left, NO price on it, opens a screen of cards): PONCHO PARADE ~80x, PIÑATA LINK ~60x (starts with 6 piñatas), PARTY PACK ~180x (Parade starting with 3 sticky ponchos). Exact prices come from the math later. Buy plays a trigger spin that drops the scatters/piñatas, then splash.
- **No MAX LUCK** (used by slots 1-2; variety rule, keep rare).
- Shared: big-win tiers, gold MAX WIN screen, turbo, autoplay, themed music (cumbia/mariachi synth loop, bonus track, stingers), 30 currencies, phone mode.

## Variety check
Main mechanic = paylines on spinning reels (1: cluster, 2: ways, 3: tins, 4: sweeps, 5: chain). Money feature = Hold & Win (nobody has it). Bonus = sticky wild ladder (differs from heat, drift, kite, aurora, chain). Extras = bet-up + 3 buys (no MAX LUCK, no ante-sweeps).

## How a spin plays out (clarity)
1. Tap SPIN: Lucho on the reels rocks back (anticipation), reels spin down and stop left to right with a small bounce.
2. Wins: winning line draws a thick colored line across the cells, winning symbols do their own win motion (Lucho dances, skull grins), a callout shows "LINE 7 - 3x TACO - $2.50" and the WIN counter counts up.
3. Piñatas: each landing piñata pops its value with a "pop" sound; at 4 or 5 piñatas the reels tease slowly toward the 6th; 6th triggers a "PIÑATA LINK" splash (tap to continue).
4. Link: counter "RESPINS 3", landed piñatas lock with a ring, non-lockers spin; each crack adds to the TOTAL banner; jackpots show a gold plaque.
5. Parade: wilds land with a poncho flourish, a LADDER bar on the left lights the next step "x3 MULTIPLIER", spins left counter on top-left, WIN counter.
6. Big wins use shared tiers; every event has text plus sound.

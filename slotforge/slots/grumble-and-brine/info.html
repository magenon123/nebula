5 reels x 3 rows, 243 WAYS. Match 3 or more of a symbol on neighbouring reels from the left; any position on a reel counts. Old Boot and Tin Can need 4 or more. Max win [[maxWin]]x your bet.

LANTERN JELLY (WILD)Jellies carry a number. A Jelly of value v counts as v copies of the symbol on its reel, and counts add up inside a reel. It substitutes for every symbol except Sonar Buoys.
THE DRIFTWhile a Jelly is on the board it drifts one reel to the left and grows by 1. Every other symbol respins and every board is paid. No win is needed to keep drifting. The Jelly leaves after it is paid on reel 1. Up to 4 drifts per spin.
SONAR BUOYS (SCATTER)3, 4 or 5 Buoys anywhere pay 2x, 10x or 50x your bet and start the DEEP DIVE with 8, 10 or 12 free dives.
DEEP DIVEEvery 3rd dive brings a guaranteed Jelly. Each Jelly that leaves raises the TIDE (max 3), and new Jellies start bigger. 2 Buoys add 3 dives, 3 or more add 6. At most 40 dives.
DEEP PRESSUREEvery spin costs [[anteCost]]x your bet, and the Deep Dive becomes about 3.3 times as likely to trigger.
BONUS BUYDIVE TICKET: buy the Deep Dive for [[buy:dive]]x. ABYSS PASS: [[buy:abyss]]x for 12 dives with Tide +2 and a Jelly on every dive. This is not the same as a natural 5-Buoy start.
